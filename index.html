Scoring

  • During play:
    • 15 exactly: 2 pts
    • 31 exactly: 2 pts
    • Go: 1 pt
    • Pair: 2 pts
    • Run of 3+: 1 pt per card
  • In hand / crib:
    • 15s: 2 pts
    • Pairs: 2 pts
    • Runs of 3+: 1 pt per card
    • Flush: 1 pt per card
    • Nobs: 1 pt

Rules

  • Deal 6 cards (5 cards in 3-player games)
  • Discard 2 cards to the crib (1 in 3-player)
  • Reveal starter card
  • Play to 31
  • Play hands (& crib if dealer)
  • First to 121 points wins
